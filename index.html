About

A thoughtful, evidence-informed approach to psychological support.

I am a psychology professional with an MSc in Clinical Psychology and experience across community, hospital, youth and workplace settings. My work centres on understanding the person as a whole and translating psychological knowledge into practical, meaningful support.

How I Work

Collaborative, individualised and grounded in evidence.

Therapeutic work begins with understanding your needs, strengths and goals. Together, we identify useful ways forward, drawing on evidence-informed psychological approaches and adapting the work to your context.

Listen & Understand

Creating space to understand what brings you to support and what you want to change.

Assess & Formulate

Using psychological assessment and formulation to build a clearer picture of your needs.

Support Meaningful Change

Developing practical skills, insight and strategies that can support progress beyond sessions.

Experience

Experience across clinical, community and workplace settings.

Professional experience includes youth and community mental health, hospital-based psychological support, wellbeing coaching and corporate Employee Assistance Programme environments. This breadth informs a practical, person-centred approach to psychological work.

Corporate Wellbeing

Supporting healthier, more resilient workplaces.

Workplace wellbeing support can help employees develop practical strategies for managing stress, strengthening resilience and maintaining psychological wellbeing. Services can be shaped around organisational needs and delivered through appropriate workplace or EAP arrangements.
